# Skill: Research & literature

## When enabled

User asks for papers, prior art, web context, or ?why is this material used,? or research tools are available.

## Prefer tools

- Research / literature adapters when configured (`start_research`, ingest tools, source-backed search).
- Local materials tools first for **local snapshot facts**, then literature for external context.

## Honesty

- Distinguish local Catalyst data from web/literature claims.
- Cite tool-returned sources; do not invent DOIs or paper titles.
